Chromatographic responses

Read directly/imported: integrated peak table, peak retention times, peak height/area and pressure trace. Derived by the tool: accepted peak count after the integration threshold, last-peak tR, worst adjacent-pair Rs selection, pass/fail against the user specifications, retention model/minimum %B where enough comparable data exist, and the next-run decision.

Using a recommendation at the instrument: the decision card identifies the parameter being changed, the controlling module and the applicable range/limit. Instrument- or column-specific maxima are not guessed.

Flow Chart 1 logic

Rubric test order: back pressure → peak count → last-peak retention time → worst adjacent-pair resolution. Excess peaks trigger INVESTIGATE. All specifications are user-set.

Course clarification: FC1 checks back pressure → peak count → last-peak tR → worst adjacent Rs. If peak count equals the user-specified target and tR/Rs both meet specification, the method is MET. If peak count equals target but either tR or Rs does not meet specification, Retention and Selectivity are frozen and the tool hands off to Efficiency (N / Flow Chart 2). No Rs floor or N calculation is required in FC1.

For fewer peaks, retention proceeds in 10% B steps while tR is at most half the run-time specification. At the ladder limit, inspect minimum %B, optional intermediate %B, then choose the retention run for selectivity. Selectivity sequence: ambient → 40°C → 60°C; second solvent at automatically calculated nomogram-equivalent %B, then ambient → 40°C → 60°C; optional mixed-solvent stage is not implemented; ligand change restarts retention.

Prototype boundaries: A raw trace without integrated peak-table metadata cannot yet yield validated Rs or pressure. This version calculates minimum %B from at least three comparable retention runs, preferring a local high-R² log k vs %B fit when t₀ is available and automatically excluding high-%B points when that improves the fit. It does not invent missing measurements or automatically assign co-eluting compounds. Confirm all suggested conditions before using an instrument.
