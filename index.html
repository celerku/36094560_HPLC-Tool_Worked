Chromatographic responses

Read directly/imported: integrated peak table, peak retention times, peak height/area and pressure trace. Derived by the tool: accepted peak count after the integration threshold, last-peak tR, worst adjacent-pair Rs selection, pass/fail against the user specifications, retention model/minimum %B where enough comparable data exist, and the next-run decision.

Using a recommendation at the instrument: the decision card identifies the parameter being changed, the controlling module and the applicable range/limit. Instrument- or column-specific maxima are not guessed.

Flow Chart 1 logic

Rubric test order: back pressure → peak count → last-peak retention time → worst adjacent-pair resolution. Excess peaks trigger INVESTIGATE. All specifications are user-set.

Decision rules: The tool checks specifications and source measurements before evaluating the sample. When the target peak count is reached, time and resolution passing gives METHOD MET; any time or resolution shortfall gives Efficiency (N). Preserve column/ligand, solvent, %B and temperature at the handoff. Blank/control injections do not advance method development. Excessive pressure is highlighted separately and must be reviewed before another injection.

For fewer peaks, retention proceeds in 10% B steps while tR is at most half the run-time specification. Above half the time limit, a full decrement is blocked. If a documented log k model is available, propose its minimum experiment; otherwise OBSERVE measured evidence. Optional intermediate experiments remain unmeasured until uploaded. Explicitly select a measured starting run for temperature. Selectivity starts at 40°C; OBSERVE measured peak movement before choosing 60°C or a nomogram modifier change; second solvent at automatically calculated nomogram-equivalent %B, then Ambient → 40°C → OBSERVE, with 60°C if movement is favourable; optional mixed-solvent stage is not implemented; ligand change restarts retention.

Prototype boundaries: A raw trace without integrated peak-table metadata cannot yet yield validated Rs or pressure. This version fits last-compound log k only with documented compound identity, justified marker t₀ and at least four comparable compositions. Fits must have negative slope and R² at least 0.99. Any excluded highest-%B points and extrapolation are reported. It does not invent missing measurements or automatically assign co-eluting compounds.
